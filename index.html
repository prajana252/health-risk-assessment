PERSONALISED ACTION IDEAS

Action studio · practical, not prescriptive

Choose a focus area to see general, low-risk ideas. These suggestions do not treat disease and should be adapted to your circumstances and any professional advice.

↗

Make movement easier

Start with a manageable walk, stretch or movement break. Increase gradually and choose an activity that suits your ability.

TRY: 10 minutes after a meal, if appropriate.
☾

Build a sleep cue

Keep a consistent wind-down time, reduce late caffeine if it affects you, and make your sleep space comfortable.

TRY: one repeatable pre-sleep ritual.
◈

Add, don't just restrict

Include a variety of vegetables, fruit, fibre-rich foods and protein sources where available. Avoid extreme diets.

TRY: add one nourishing food to a meal.
◉

Hydration check-in

Drink regularly and consider thirst, heat, activity and any clinician-provided fluid restrictions.

TRY: keep water accessible during the day.
✳

Make space to reset

Try a short breathing pause, journaling, time outdoors or talking with someone you trust. Seek support if distress persists.

TRY: schedule a five-minute pause.
＋

Bring measurements to a clinician

Use properly measured readings and ask what follow-up, if any, is appropriate. Don't change medication based on this demo.

TRY: keep a dated record of readings.
ⓘ
Safety first

Do not start, stop or change medication based on this website. New, severe or urgent symptoms need prompt medical attention.
